Why 25%?

Both 20% and 25% satisfy the project's validation constraints of at least 85% fraud capture and no more than 2% false-positive rate. We selected 25% because it improves precision from 77.31% to 79.98%, reduces the false-positive rate from 1.06% to 0.90%, and improves F1 from 81.86% to 82.82%, while recall decreases by only 1 percentage point, from 86.88% to 85.88%.

This represents RazorShield's preferred operational balance on the held-out evaluation set, not a universal payment-industry rule.

Lower Threshold More Fraud Captured Higher recall, but more legitimate transactions may be flagged.
